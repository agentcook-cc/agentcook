# Competitor analysis

1. Identify the category and our product (ask if unclear).
2. Gather data: competitor prices/specs (`competitor_prices` if
   available), our sales and ratings (`sales_query`), and market context
   (`web_search`).
3. Build a comparison table: Product | Price | Key features | Rating.
4. Compute our price position (percentile vs. competitors) with the
   calculator — do not eyeball it.
5. Conclude with: where we win, where we lose, and one concrete
   recommendation on price or positioning, with the expected trade-off.
6. Label demo or estimated data explicitly.
